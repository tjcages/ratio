## Ratio 0.4.0

- **Settings panel.** The settings button in the bottom-right corner replaces the light/dark toggle. It holds **Open at login** and **Appearance** (dark or light).
- **Opens at login by default.** On first launch Ratio adds itself to your login items. Turn it off in Settings and it stays off.
- **Browser pages.** Expand a browser to see and classify its individual pages. Pages inherit the browser's setting unless you override them.
- **Usage controls.** Right-click an app or page to hide it from the list or delete today's usage, with undo.
- **Scrolling titles** for long app and page names.
- No purchase prompts or paid-update sign-in.

### Install

Download **Ratio.dmg**, open it, and drag Ratio into Applications. This build isn't notarized by Apple, so the first launch is blocked: open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. On macOS 14 and earlier you can instead right-click Ratio and choose **Open**.

Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12 or newer. Open at login needs macOS 13 or newer.
